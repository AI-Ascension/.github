# Code of Conduct

AI-Ascension is a small project that argues from evidence. We hold each other to that standard
and to ordinary decency.

## What we expect

- Challenge claims, code, and reasoning with evidence; treat the people behind them with patience.
- State your assumptions and label what you have not verified.
- Accept correction and correct yourself in public when you were wrong.
- Give review feedback that someone can act on.
- Respect privacy and consent: no one's saves, paths, identifiers, or messages appear in an issue
  without their agreement.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and for fixing them.

## What we do not accept

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, publishing private
information, malicious disruption of the project's spaces, or retaliation against anyone who reports
a concern in good faith.

## Where this applies

Every `AI-Ascension` repository, issue tracker, review thread, and any public place where someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently
exclude a participant. Responses are proportionate to the conduct and the risk, and are recorded
privately.

## Reporting

Report a concern through the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Neither the
reporter nor the subject is required to argue sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers keep
reports as confidential as practical and step aside from a report in which they have a conflict of
interest.
